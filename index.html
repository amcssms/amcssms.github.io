SSMS Mess Portal

Society for Student Mess Services · BITS Pilani, Pilani campus

What it is

SSMS Mess Portal is the meal sign-up system run by the Society for Student Mess Services at BITS Pilani. Students book the items they want each day, and our food service provider receives the final list so meals can be prepared in the right quantities.

Open the portal

How it works

  • Students sign in with their @pilani.bits-pilani.ac.in Google account, enter their BITS ID and name once, and book Millet Bowl, Boiled Chicken, or both, up to 7 days ahead.
  • Same-day bookings and cancellations close at 9:00 AM IST.
  • At 10:00 AM IST the food service provider receives an Excel list of that day's orders: each student's BITS ID, name and item(s).
  • The mess committee manages menus, students and reports from an admin dashboard.

Your data

The portal uses Google Sign-In only to confirm your email address. We use your details only to run the mess service and never sell them or use them for advertising. Read the Privacy Policy for full details.
