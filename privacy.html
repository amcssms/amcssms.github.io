Privacy Policy

SSMS Mess Portal · Society for Student Mess Services, BITS Pilani · Effective 11 November 2026

1. Who we are

The SSMS Mess Portal ("the portal") is operated by the Society for Student Mess Services ("SSMS", "we") at BITS Pilani, Pilani campus. It lets students sign up for meals and gives our food service provider the daily list of orders. Contact: amc@ssms-pilani.com.

2. Information we collect

From Google Sign-In. When you sign in with Google, Google shares your email address, name and profile picture with the portal. We use only your email address, to confirm that you hold a @pilani.bits-pilani.ac.in account (or are the committee administrator), and we store only the email address. We do not request access to your Gmail, Drive, contacts, calendar or any other Google data.

Information you enter. Your BITS ID, full name and, optionally, phone number and bhawan (hostel).

Your bookings. The dates and items (e.g. Millet Bowl, Boiled Chicken) you sign up for, and when you made or changed each booking.

Technical information. A random sign-in token stored in your browser so you stay signed in, and an activity log of actions in the portal (for example, sign-ins and booking changes) with timestamps.

3. How we use it

  • To let you sign in and book or cancel meals.
  • To prepare the daily order list for our food service provider.
  • To run the mess service: menu planning, meal counts, resolving disputes and managing accounts (for example, pausing an account).

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling or any purpose unrelated to the mess service, and we do not sell it.

4. Who we share it with

  • Our food service provider sees, for each day, the BITS ID, name and item(s) of students who booked. It does not see your email, phone number or booking history.
  • The SSMS mess committee administrator can see all portal data in order to run the service.
  • Google hosts the portal and its data (Google Apps Script, Google Sheets, Google Drive and Gmail, under the committee's Google account) and processes it under Google's terms.

We share information with anyone else only if required by law or by BITS Pilani regulations.

5. Google user data

The portal's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data received from Google Sign-In (your email address) is used only to verify your identity for the portal, is not transferred to anyone except as described in section 4, and is not used for advertising.

6. Storage, security and retention

Data is stored in a Google Sheet and Google Drive folder owned by the committee account amc@ssms-pilani.com. Access is limited to the committee administrator. Food service provider passwords are stored only as salted hashes. Sign-in sessions expire after 30 days (7 days for the administrator).

We keep booking records for the current academic semester, plus up to one further semester for accounting. After that they are deleted or anonymised. Daily order reports kept in Drive follow the same schedule.

7. Your choices and rights

  • You can view and edit your BITS ID, name, phone and bhawan in the portal at any time.
  • You can ask us to delete your account and data by emailing amc@ssms-pilani.com from your BITS email. We will do so within 14 days, except for records we must keep for open accounting periods.
  • You can sign out at any time, and you can revoke the portal's sign-in access in your Google Account under Security → Third-party apps & services.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above and, for significant changes, announce it in the portal.
